# Anticoagulants and analgesics

Demo corpus summarised from widely published general knowledge. Not clinical guidance.

## NSAIDs and warfarin
NSAIDs such as ibuprofen, naproxen and aspirin raise bleeding risk, including GI bleeding, in patients on warfarin or other anticoagulants. Acetaminophen (paracetamol) is generally the preferred simple analgesic, though regular high doses can raise INR in patients on warfarin.

## Antibiotics and INR
Several antibiotics can raise INR in patients taking warfarin. INR should be checked within a few days of starting treatment.

## SSRIs and bleeding
SSRIs and SNRIs impair platelet function and add to bleeding risk when combined with anticoagulants or NSAIDs.
