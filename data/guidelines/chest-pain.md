# Chest pain

Demo corpus summarised from widely published general knowledge. Not clinical guidance.

## Features that need emergency assessment
Pressure, tightness or heaviness in the chest, pain spreading to the arm, jaw or back, shortness of breath, sweating, nausea, or fainting suggest a possible acute coronary syndrome. Sudden tearing pain radiating to the back, or sudden breathlessness with pleuritic pain, raises concern for aortic dissection or pulmonary embolism. These patients should be sent for emergency care rather than managed in a routine visit.

## Lower risk features
Pain that is sharp, reproducible on pressing the chest wall, or related to position or breathing is more often musculoskeletal, but this does not exclude a cardiac cause in patients with risk factors.
