# Urinary tract infection

Demo corpus summarised from widely published general knowledge. Not clinical guidance.

## Uncomplicated cystitis
Typical symptoms are burning on urination, frequency and urgency without fever or flank pain. A short antibiotic course is usual, chosen with local resistance patterns and allergy history in mind.

## Red flags
Fever, rigors, flank pain, vomiting, or symptoms in pregnancy, in men, or in patients with diabetes or kidney disease may indicate a complicated infection or pyelonephritis and need urine culture and closer assessment.
