# Diabetes and hypertension follow-up

Demo corpus summarised from widely published general knowledge. Not clinical guidance.

## Metformin
Metformin is contraindicated when eGFR is below 30 and should not usually be started when eGFR is between 30 and 45. Kidney function should be checked at least yearly. Gastrointestinal upset is common early on.

## Type 2 diabetes review
A routine review usually covers HbA1c, home glucose readings, foot and eye screening, kidney function, blood pressure and lipid management.

## Hypertension
Blood pressure should be confirmed with repeated readings, ideally including home or ambulatory measurement. ACE inhibitors can cause a dry cough, and potassium and kidney function should be checked after starting or changing the dose. NSAIDs can blunt the effect of antihypertensives.
